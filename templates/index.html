DWT Fusion Methodology

The proposed system performs multi-scale decomposition and combines important information from CT and MRI images.

01. Decomposition

CT and MRI images are decomposed using the Discrete Wavelet Transform.

02. LL Fusion

Low-frequency coefficients are combined using averaging.

03. Detail Fusion

High-frequency coefficients are selected according to their absolute coefficient strength.

04. Reconstruction

The inverse DWT reconstructs the final fused medical image.

About Project

This Digital Image Processing project demonstrates DWT-based medical image fusion. CT images provide valuable structural information while MRI images provide complementary soft-tissue information. The fusion process combines these complementary features into a single image.

The system also calculates image quality measures such as SSIM, PSNR and Entropy to support quantitative evaluation of the fused result.
