Discrete Wavelet Transform

DWT decomposes an image into different frequency components. The four major sub-bands are LL, LH, HL and HH.

LL - Approximation

LL Component
Contains the main low-frequency and structural information.

LH - Horizontal Details

LH Component
Represents horizontal detail information in the image.

HL - Vertical Details

HL Component
Represents vertical detail information in the image.

HH - Diagonal Details

HH Component
Represents diagonal and high-frequency details.

Fusion Process

CT and MRI images are decomposed into multi-scale frequency components. The corresponding coefficients are then combined and reconstructed using the inverse DWT to produce the fused image.
